Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Draft — owner review required. Before public launch, the operator must add a legal identity and monitored contact address, confirm actual data practices, and review applicable law. This is not legal advice.

1. Operator

TRADE AR MONEY is an educational community app and website. The operator’s legal name and privacy contact address must be added before public launch.

2. Information processed

  • Account information: email address, authentication user identifier, and security metadata handled by Supabase Authentication.
  • Content: learning articles, announcements, support messages, and history records published or submitted through available features.
  • Technical data: information required by hosting, authentication, database, and content-delivery providers to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Practice interactions: ARG V1 BIG/SMALL selections and a local timer may be displayed as practice activity, not verified predictions.

Do not put passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in public content or support fields.

3. Purposes

Information may be used to provide and secure accounts, display educational content, administer the community, respond to support requests, and diagnose technical problems or abuse.

4. Providers and disclosure

The project is configured to use Supabase for authentication and database services and a third-party CDN to load the Supabase JavaScript client on the website. Providers may process technical information under their own terms. Additional providers must be documented before they are added.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the service and its users, or as part of a legitimate transfer of the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and content records may remain in the configured backend until deleted or covered by a retention process. The operator must configure and test account deletion, backups, and retention. A monitored contact address for access or deletion requests must be added before launch. Never send passwords or access tokens with such a request.

6. Security

The project is designed to use Supabase authentication and database access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. The operator must test row-level security, admin permissions, backups, and secret handling. Server-only secrets must never be embedded in the app, public website, or repository.

7. Children

The operator must decide the intended age range and apply any required parental-consent and child-privacy safeguards before accepting users who may be minors.

8. International processing

Hosting and service providers may process data in other locations. The operator should confirm current processing locations and applicable transfer requirements.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The operator should publish an updated effective date and notify users when required.

10. Contact and launch requirements

Before public launch, add the operator’s legal identity and a monitored privacy contact, confirm actual data flows, configure deletion and retention, review provider terms, and obtain appropriate legal review.
